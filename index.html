All about mental health

Whole health begins with mental health

Perhaps you're feeling down or anxious, struggling with substance use, or sensing that you aren't living the life you were meant to lead. Whatever your needs may be, we're here to offer support — and we understand that reaching out can be the hardest step.

At Polaris Mental Health Services, we adopt a patient-first philosophy. We aim to exceed expectations, one individual and one community at a time, delivering comprehensive psychiatric care in a private, compassionate, and friendly atmosphere.

Dr. Christabel Lartey is a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner with a Doctor of Nursing Practice degree from George Mason University. She holds board certification through the American Nurses Credentialing Center (ANCC).

Dr. Lartey sees each patient as a unique individual and considers their whole circumstances when evaluating mental health needs. She discusses treatment options openly, builds an individualized care plan, and works alongside patients to reach their goals.

She makes a point of listening and understanding a patient's needs beyond simply prescribing medication, treating decision-making as collaborative and educating patients throughout the process as she combines medication and psychotherapy for the best results.

Current medications

Please bring — or have a way to access — all prescribed medications at your first appointment. Your provider will prescribe enough medication to last until your next scheduled medication management appointment. If you miss a scheduled appointment, refills are at the provider's discretion, so please keep your scheduled appointments.

Cancellations & no-show policy

We require a minimum of 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment and avoid a fee. Appointments missed, cancelled, or rescheduled with less than 48 hours' notice incur a $75 fee, which is not covered by insurance and will be charged to the credit card on file.

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your appointment will be cancelled and the cancellation fee assessed.

Treatment dismissal policy

You have the right to end care at any time and for any reason. We reserve the right to end treatment if:

  • We suspect prescription medication abuse or undisclosed substance use
  • A prescription drug contract is violated during treatment
  • We believe our services are no longer beneficial to you
  • We believe you would be better served by a higher level of care or a different provider
  • There is a pattern of frequent cancellations, rescheduling, or no-shows
  • Interpersonal dynamics are not conducive to a therapeutic relationship
  • You or a family member are hostile or aggressive toward providers, staff, or other clients
Emergencies & medication refills

In a psychiatric emergency, go to your local Emergency Department, call 911, or call/text 988. Our crisis resources page lists national and Prince William County lines.

Medications are not refilled outside of business hours, including holidays, nights, and weekends. Please allow 72 hours (excluding weekends and holidays) for refill requests, and request refills in a timely manner. We reserve the right to decline refills at any time.

Crisis resources

Immediate help is available

If you or someone else may be in danger, the resources below can connect you with trained support right now — day or night, free and confidential.

In immediate danger? Don't wait. Call 911 Call or text 988

National crisis lines

Free, confidential, and available 24 hours a day, every day.

24/7 · Free · Confidential

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988 Chat online at 988lifeline.org

Connects you with a trained crisis counselor for thoughts of suicide, emotional distress, substance use concerns, or any mental health crisis. You can also call on behalf of someone you're worried about. Press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line, 2 for Spanish, or 3 for LGBTQI+ support.

24/7 · Free · Confidential

Crisis Text Line

Text HOME to 741741

Text-based support with a trained crisis counselor. A good option if talking on the phone feels like too much, or if you need to reach out discreetly.

24/7 · Free · Chat

TheHopeLine

thehopeline.com

One-on-one chat with a trained HopeCoach, available around the clock at no cost, for students and young adults facing depression, anxiety, grief, loneliness, bullying, substance use, and more. This is a faith-based Christian organization; coaches offer a listening ear and referrals, and prayer is available if you want it.

24/7

Veterans Crisis Line

Call 988, then press 1 Text 838255

Confidential support for veterans, service members, National Guard and Reserve, and their families and friends. You do not need to be enrolled in VA benefits or health care.

24/7

The Trevor Project

1-866-488-7386 Text START to 678-678

Crisis intervention and suicide prevention for LGBTQ+ young people under 25, staffed by counselors trained in LGBTQ+ affirming support.

24/7

SAMHSA National Helpline

1-800-662-HELP (4357)

Treatment referral and information for mental health and substance use disorders, in English and Spanish. This is a referral line rather than crisis counseling — use 988 for a crisis — but it is excellent for finding treatment.

24/7

National Domestic Violence Hotline

1-800-799-7233 Text START to 88788

Support, safety planning, and local referrals for anyone experiencing or worried about abuse in a relationship.

Local help in Prince William County and Manassas

Local lines connect you with responders who know the area and can refer you to nearby services.

24/7 · Local

CR2 — Community Regional Crisis Response

703-527-4077 Text CONNECT to 85511

Mobile crisis response serving Prince William County, Manassas, Manassas Park, and surrounding Northern Virginia communities. Trained responders can help over the phone or come to you when someone is in crisis but not in immediate danger.

Prince William County Community Services — Emergency Services

Manassas: 703-792-7800 Woodbridge: 703-792-4900

Round-the-clock crisis intervention and emergency evaluations for behavioral health and substance use crises. Walk-in and scheduled services are available at the Sudley North office, 7969 Ashton Ave., Manassas. TTY: 711.

UVA Health Prince William Medical Center, Manassas UVA Health Haymarket Medical Center, Haymarket Sentara Northern Virginia Medical Center, Woodbridge

Peer support, education, and family programs for people living with mental illness and those who care for them. The HelpLine offers information and referrals during weekday hours rather than crisis counseling.

Eating disorder support

The National Alliance for Eating Disorders operates a clinician-staffed helpline on weekdays offering support and treatment referrals for anorexia, bulimia, binge eating, and all other eating disorders. Sexual and reproductive health care, including contraception, STI testing and treatment, pregnancy testing and options counseling, and general wellness services, with sliding-scale fees at many locations. Several health centers serve Northern Virginia.

Poison Control

1-800-222-1222

Immediate guidance for any suspected overdose, poisoning, or serious medication reaction.

What happens when you call a crisis line

  • A trained counselor answers and listens. You lead the conversation and share only what you want to share.
  • Calls are confidential, and you are never required to give your name.
  • Most calls end with a plan and a next step, not an ambulance. Emergency services are involved only when there is imminent danger to life.
  • You can call about someone else. If you are worried about a friend or family member, these lines will help you decide what to do.
  • There is no threshold you have to meet. You do not have to be in danger to deserve support.
